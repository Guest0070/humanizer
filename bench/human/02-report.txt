The council received 214 objections to the plan, most of them about parking. Residents on Hale Street said the proposed 40 spaces would not cover the 60 flats, and the transport officer agreed the figure was low. The developer has since offered 52 spaces and a car club bay.

Two objections raised flooding. The site sits above the 1953 flood line, but the drainage report was written before the culvert collapsed in March, so the officer has asked for a new one. The committee will decide on 14 November.
