Most teams don't have a speed problem. They have a focus problem.

Here's what I mean. You ship a feature. Then another. Then another. And somehow, nothing moves. Not revenue. Not retention. Nothing.

The fix isn't more meetings. It's fewer priorities.

Pick one metric. Just one. Make every decision answer to it. Say no to everything else — even the good ideas. Especially the good ideas.

It sounds simple. It is simple. But simple isn't easy.

That's the real lesson. Focus is a skill, not a slogan. And like any skill, it gets better with practice.
