I bought this for the battery and stayed for the keyboard. The battery is fine: about nine hours of email and browsing, closer to five if I'm compiling anything. The keyboard is the best I've used on a laptop, with deep travel and no rattle.

The screen is the weak spot. It's dim outdoors and the matte coating makes text look slightly grainy, which you stop noticing after a week. The fan is loud under load. If you mostly write, buy it. If you edit video, look elsewhere.
