We moved the kiln on Saturday. It took four of us and most of the morning, mainly because nobody had measured the side gate. It was two inches too narrow. Tom wanted to take the gate off its hinges; I wanted to tip the kiln on its side. We did both in the end, which is probably why my back hurts.

The new spot is better. It gets afternoon shade, so the shed doesn't cook in July, and the cable run to the fuse box is half what it was. Still, I'm not convinced the floor is level. The first glaze firing will tell us.
